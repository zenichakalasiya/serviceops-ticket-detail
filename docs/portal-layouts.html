What to ship first

Two layouts that genuinely differ prove the template system does something. One layout plus a recolour cannot.

L2 · Search Spotlight

Built. The only one that changes the page’s question rather than its decoration, and the cheapest to defend — every reference leads with a field. The ITSM-specific move is that the requester’s own work stays on the page, which is what separates it from a help centre.

L4 · Status Board

Its opposite. It serves the daily user the current portal actively fails: someone who knows what they want and must read past a hero, four doors and two service rows to find out that two approvals are waiting.

Not L6 or L9 first, however well they review. Both score 2 or lower on sparse-tenant resilience — and the first tenant to open one with no announcements and no assets will conclude the product is broken rather than that their data is empty.
