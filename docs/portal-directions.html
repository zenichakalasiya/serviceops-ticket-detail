Twelve Portal Directions

Not twelve arrangements of the same page — twelve identities. Each pairs a structure with the banner treatment, palette, card anatomy and density that its tenant's domain actually calls for. Same components throughout: if any of these needed its own markup, they would not be one system.

Reading these

Three things are worth knowing before you pick.

Radius is a dialect2px reads as a form, 16px reads as an app. It is the cheapest signal of what kind of place this is, and it is one token.
Density is domainFinance and Manufacturing get 8px gaps and 4px radii; Healthcare and University get room. Nobody reads a shift-handover page the way they read a course catalogue.
Four carry a hard ban03 no equal-card grid · 08 inverted order, banner last · 11 half height and no search · 12 no banner at all. A ban is the only reliable way to find out whether an element was ever load-bearing.
