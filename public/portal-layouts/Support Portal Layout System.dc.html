ServiceOps · Support Portal · 14 layouts
Fourteen layouts, fourteen ways to raise a ticket
Newest at the top. Every layout keeps the incident action as its primary move but treats it differently: an amber CTA on the banner photo (4b), a solid button beside the welcome search (4a), a raise-a-ticket card beside a segmented search bar (3i), a promo action band beside a services rail (3h), four cards tucked into the banner's right side (3g), a numbered keypad (3d), a pinned ribbon (3e), one grouped list card (3f), edge slabs (3a), a sticky vertical rail (3b), glass tiles inside the banner (3c), a weighted bento (2a), a pill bar under the data (2b), typed intent chips (2c). Taxonomy, section specs, grouping maps and state behaviour live in the spec doc. Still to come: the domain matrix and the builder primitives list.

A photographic banner with the announcement carousel floating over its right edge, a topic strip, then a filterable catalog beside a countdown and quick-links rail.
