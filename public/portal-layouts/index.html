Support Portal — Layout System

Fourteen layout directions for the requester's landing page, drawn as full 1440px artboards. Every card below opens the canvas scrolled to that layout.

Open the full canvas Layout spec Templates built in the product

14 layouts

2aPrism

Split banner — brand colour and geometry on the right, all the words and the search on the left, so contrast is never at risk.
2bLedger

No banner — the colour block at the top is made of the tenant's own numbers, and every widget below it lives in one table.
2cFront Door

Search is the page — quick actions become typed intents on a patterned ground, and everything you own is one bento below the fold.
3aMarquee

Duotone banner, and three wide action slabs straddling its edge — the incident slab is twice the width of the other two.
3bSidecar

Actions live in a sticky vertical rail that never scrolls away — the incident card sits at the top of it, filled.
3cCounter

Actions sit inside the banner as glass tiles on solid brand colour, with the incident tile inverted to white.
3dSwitchboard

A numbered keypad of oversized action tiles — built for gloved hands, shared terminals and a shift worker who has 20 seconds.
3eRibbon

A media slider replaces the banner, and the actions move into a ribbon pinned under the top bar so they survive the scroll.
3fStack

One 820px column, no grid at all — actions are a single grouped card and everything else is a dated feed, so a thin tenant simply has a shorter page.
3gFoyer

A compact banner with the four actions tucked into its right side, then a hard split: what's mine on the left, what's published on the right.
3hConcierge

A greeting strip with inline counters, service cards you favourite like products, and a master–detail panel where a request opens beside its own timeline.
3iWayfinder

Minimal and airy — a sub-nav strip, a left-aligned hero with a segmented search bar, and flat bordered cards with no shadows anywhere.
4aService Center

A promo slider beside a welcome panel, a mosaic of feature tiles, then a two-column service directory with a tasks-and-links rail.
4bBroadsheet

A photographic banner with the announcement carousel floating over its right edge, a topic strip, then a filterable catalog beside a countdown and quick-links rail.
